Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to HTML Mastery (accessible via https://webmastery-v1.surge.sh/). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be collected while you use our educational website. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, how cookies and third-party advertising services operate, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

HTML Mastery is designed to be completely usable without creating an account or providing personal identity details. We do not require, collect, or store your name, password, phone number, or billing information on any backend database server.

3. Local Storage (On-Device Browser Storage)

To provide a persistent, seamless learning experience across study sessions, HTML Mastery uses your web browser's standard localStorage API. This data stays entirely on your local device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Curriculum Progress: Completed lesson checkboxes, overall completion percentages, and milestone badges.
  • Gamification & Stats: Experience Points (XP), current learning streak, and learner level.
  • Preferences: Dark or light UI theme selection and focus mode toggles.
  • Saved Code: Any HTML/CSS code snippets you explicitly save within lesson exercise editors.
  • Bookmarks: Saved lessons for quick access from the dashboard.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or cache.

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Third-Party Advertising

Like most modern websites, third-party services and advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons to improve user experience and deliver relevant advertisements.

Google AdSense

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and its network of certified third-party ad vendors. Here is what you should know:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to HTML Mastery and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative consumer opt-out page.

5. External Content & CDNs

To ensure fast page loads and consistent typography, HTML Mastery loads fonts via Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When your browser fetches these resources, your IP address and standard HTTP request headers may be received by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area or California), you have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Since all learning data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage, you have direct, instantaneous access to your data at any time.
  • Right to Deletion: You can erase all stored data by clearing browser storage or resetting progress via the dashboard.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

7. Children's Information (COPPA)

HTML Mastery is an educational website intended for general audiences interested in learning web development. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that such information has been provided, please contact us immediately so we can ensure its prompt removal.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technical features, legal requirements, or advertising partners. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
