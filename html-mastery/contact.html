Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is HTML Mastery completely free to use?

Yes. All 47 HTML lessons, 49 CSS topics, interactive visual playgrounds, code editors, and guided projects are 100% free and open for personal learning.

How is my learning progress saved?

Your lesson completion checkboxes, daily streaks, XP points, and saved code exercises are stored locally on your device using the browser's localStorage API. We do not require account creation, and your learning data remains private on your computer.

Can I use code examples from HTML Mastery in my own projects?

Absolutely! All code examples, syntax patterns, and starter templates provided across lessons and projects are meant for educational use and can freely be adapted into your personal or professional projects.

How can I contribute or report an issue?

You can send an email to abhimanyukumar1502@gmail.com with the page title, error description, and your suggested fix. We review every submission.
